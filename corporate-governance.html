Corporate Services

Corporate Governance

Board structures, shareholder agreements, and ongoing compliance

Strong corporate governance is the backbone of any sustainable business. It defines how a company is directed, controlled, and held accountable — protecting shareholders, aligning management, and building investor confidence.

Our firm advises corporate entities on designing and maintaining effective governance frameworks aligned with Egyptian law and international best practices. From board structures and shareholder agreements to general assemblies and ongoing compliance, we help you build a robust governance foundation that supports growth and minimizes risk.

What We Handle

Complete Governance Advisory

From structuring to ongoing compliance.

Board Structures

Designing and reviewing board compositions, committees, and decision-making frameworks aligned with Egyptian company law.

Shareholder Agreements

Drafting and negotiating shareholder agreements that address control, transfer restrictions, exit rights, and dispute resolution.

General Assemblies

Organizing and advising on ordinary and extraordinary general assembly meetings — including notice, quorum, and voting procedures.

Compliance Advisory

Ongoing compliance guidance for boards and executive management — regulatory filings, disclosures, and governance policies.

Corporate Disputes

Representation in shareholder disputes, director liability claims, and corporate governance litigation.

Corporate Secretarial

Maintaining corporate records, minute books, and statutory registers — ensuring your company stays in good standing.
