Entity Types

Choosing the Right Legal Form

Under Egyptian Companies Law No. 159 of 1981 and its Executive Regulations, the following legal forms are most commonly used.

Most Common

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

الشريك المسؤولية المحدودة

  • 2–50 partners
  • Minimum capital: EGP 1,000 (paid in full)
  • Liability limited to capital contributions
  • Flexible governance structure
  • Transfer of shares requires partner consent
  • Can be 100% foreign-owned in most sectors
Best for Closely held businesses, family ventures, subsidiaries, startups, and companies not planning broad public investment.
For Growth & Investment

Joint Stock Company (JSC)

شركة المساهمة

  • Minimum 3 shareholders (no maximum)
  • Minimum issued capital: EGP 250,000 (closed JSC)
  • 10% paid at incorporation, rising to 25% within 3 months
  • Managed by a Board of Directors
  • Shares freely transferable (subject to restrictions)
  • Can be listed on the Egyptian Exchange
Best for Larger investments, companies raising capital, businesses planning to list, and ventures requiring formal governance.
For Solo Founders

One Person Company (OPC)

شركة الشخص الواحد

  • Single shareholder (natural or juridical person)
  • Minimum capital: EGP 1,000 (paid in full)
  • Limited liability for the sole owner
  • Simplified governance and lower compliance
  • Can be established electronically via GAFI
  • Suitable for individual investors and entrepreneurs
Best for Sole founders, individual investors, entrepreneurs seeking limited liability without partners.

Note on foreign ownership

Under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017, foreign investors may own up to 100% of companies in most economic sectors without an Egyptian partner. Exceptions apply to certain activities — including commercial agency, importation for resale, and specific sectors requiring prior approval. Our firm advises on the specific rules applicable to your proposed activity.

Documents Required

What You'll Need to Prepare

Key documents typically required when incorporating a company through GAFI in Egypt.

  • Certificate of no name conflict from the Commercial Registry
  • Powers of attorney from all founders/shareholders
  • National ID cards or valid passports of all founders
  • Bank certificate confirming deposit of required capital percentage
  • Lease agreement or proof of company premises
  • Auditor's acceptance letter and bank certificate
  • Draft articles of association and incorporation contract
  • Security clearance application (for foreign shareholders)
  • Board resolution and corporate documents (for corporate shareholders)
  • Notarised and legalised documents (for foreign-issued documents)

Typical timeline

For a complete file, GAFI's incorporation procedures generally take between 1–2 weeks. An expedited "VIP" service is available for an additional fee. LLCs may be established in as little as one business day through GAFI's simplified electronic service for SMEs.

A JSC requires at least 3 shareholders, a minimum issued capital of EGP 250,000 (for closed companies), a Board of Directors, and is more suitable for larger investments and companies planning to raise capital or list on the Egyptian Exchange.

For a complete file, GAFI's incorporation procedures generally take between 1–2 weeks. An expedited "VIP" service is available. LLCs may be established in as little as one business day through GAFI's simplified electronic service for SMEs.

For an LLC or One Person Company, the minimum capital is EGP 1,000 (paid in full at incorporation). For a closed Joint Stock Company, the minimum issued capital is EGP 250,000 — with 10% paid at incorporation, increasing to 25% within three months. Certain regulated activities may require higher capital.

Government fees include notarisation fees (0.25% of paid-in capital, capped at EGP 10,000), GAFI service fees (0.1% of capital, with minimum EGP 1,000 and maximum EGP 10,000), commercial registry fees, and chamber of commerce fees.
