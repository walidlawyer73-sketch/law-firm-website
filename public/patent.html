Common Questions About Patents

Patents protect inventions that are new, involve an inventive step, and are industrially applicable — with a term of 20 years. Utility models protect minor inventions with lower inventive step requirements and shorter terms (typically 7–10 years). Utility models are often easier and cheaper to obtain.

Typically 3–5 years from filing to grant, depending on the technical field and examination backlog. The filing date establishes your priority — so your rights are secured from day one, even while examination is pending.

A prior art search is not strictly required — but it is strongly recommended. It helps assess whether your invention is truly novel, identify potential obstacles, and shape your claims strategically. Skipping it can result in wasted costs and rejected applications.
