Copyright Protection

Safeguard your creative and literary works.

What is Copyright?

Copyright protects original works of authorship, including literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works, such as books, software, music, and films. It gives the creator exclusive rights to reproduce, distribute, and display the work.

Our Copyright Services Include:

  • Copyright registration (Egypt and internationally)
  • Drafting and negotiating licensing agreements
  • Assignment and transfer of rights
  • Enforcement against infringement
  • DMCA takedown notices
  • Advice on fair use and public domain

Why Register Copyright?

While copyright protection exists automatically upon creation, registration provides important benefits, including a public record of your claim and the ability to sue for infringement and claim statutory damages.

Why Work With Us?

Our team understands the nuances of copyright law in the digital age. We help creators and businesses protect their valuable content and enforce their rights against unauthorized use.
