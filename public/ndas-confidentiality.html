Contracts & Agreements

NDAs & Confidentiality

Protecting what makes your business valuable — its secrets

Your confidential information — customer lists, pricing, formulas, source code, business strategies — is often your most valuable asset. Once disclosed without protection, it can be impossible to recover. NDAs and confidentiality agreements are the legal tools that keep it safe.

Our firm drafts, reviews, and enforces NDAs and confidentiality agreements for businesses, investors, employers, and individuals. Whether you're entering a negotiation, hiring employees, sharing information with vendors, or protecting trade secrets — we make sure your confidentiality is legally enforceable in Egypt and beyond.

What We Handle

Types of Confidentiality Agreements

From one-way NDAs to comprehensive trade secret programs.

One-Way NDAs

Unilateral non-disclosure agreements — used when only one party is disclosing confidential information (e.g., in acquisitions or vendor evaluations).

Mutual NDAs

Bilateral confidentiality agreements — used when both parties are sharing sensitive information (e.g., joint ventures and partnerships).

Employee Confidentiality

Employee confidentiality and invention assignment agreements — protecting trade secrets during and after employment.

Contractor & Vendor NDAs

Confidentiality agreements for consultants, freelancers, vendors, and service providers with access to your data.

Negotiation NDAs

Pre-negotiation and pre-term-sheet NDAs — protecting discussions before deals are formalized.

Trade Secret Protection

Comprehensive trade secret programs — internal policies, employee training, and enforcement procedures.

Enforcement & Litigation

Enforcement of NDAs against breach — including injunctive relief, damages claims, and criminal complaints.

Cross-Border NDAs

Bilingual NDAs with choice of law and arbitration clauses for cross-border transactions.

NDA Review & Advisory

Review of NDAs received from third parties — identifying overreaching clauses and negotiating balanced terms.
