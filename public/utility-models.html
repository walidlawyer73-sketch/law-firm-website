Utility Models

Cost-effective protection for minor innovations and incremental improvements

What is a Utility Model?

A utility model, often called a "petty patent," protects technical inventions that are new and industrially applicable but may not involve an inventive step as high as required for patents. They are ideal for incremental innovations and improvements to existing products. In Egypt, utility models are protected under the same law as patents but with simplified requirements and shorter terms.

7-Year Protection

Protection lasts for 7 years from the filing date, non-renewable.

Simpler Requirements

Novelty and industrial applicability required, but inventive step is not.

Faster Grant

Generally faster and simpler than patent prosecution.

Lower Cost

Cost-effective protection compared to standard patents.

Market Insight: Utility models are particularly popular among SMEs and startups, accounting for over 15% of IP filings in countries that offer this protection.

Our Utility Model Services Include:

  • Patentability assessment for utility model qualification
  • Application drafting (description, claims, and drawings)
  • Filing and prosecution with the Egyptian Patent Office
  • Novelty searches and prior art analysis
  • Portfolio management and maintenance
  • Conversion between patent and utility model applications
  • Enforcement against infringement

Why Consider Utility Models?

For many businesses, utility models offer a cost-effective way to protect incremental innovations. They provide exclusive rights without the lengthy and expensive examination process required for patents. With our expertise, we help you determine whether a utility model or a patent is the right strategy for your invention.
