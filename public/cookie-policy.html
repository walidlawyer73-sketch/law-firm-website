How we use cookies and similar technologies on our website

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to the website's owners.

Cookies do not contain viruses or malware, and they cannot access other information on your device. They are not programs and cannot install anything on your computer.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as local storage or pixel tags for the same purposes described in this policy. All references to "cookies" in this policy include these similar technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a limited number of purposes, all of which are aimed at making our website work properly and improving our visitors' experience:

  • Functionality: to keep the website working as expected, and to remember settings such as your preferred language.
  • Security: to help protect our website against misuse, fraud, and unauthorized activity.
  • Analytics: to understand how visitors use our website — such as which pages are most popular — so we can improve our content.
  • Performance: to help ensure our website loads quickly and functions smoothly on different devices.

We do not use cookies for advertising, and we do not track you across third-party websites.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies we use fall into three general categories:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They enable core features such as page navigation, form submission, and secure access. You cannot opt out of these cookies — without them, the website cannot function properly.

Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies collect aggregated, anonymized information about how visitors use our website — which pages are visited most, how long visitors spend on each page, and how they arrived at our site. We use this information to improve our website content and structure. These cookies do not identify you personally.

Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make — such as your preferred language (Arabic or English) — and provide enhanced, personalized features. They may also be used to remember whether you've dismissed certain notifications or banners.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are placed by third-party services that we use to support our operations. The primary third-party service we use is:

  • Google Analytics — used to understand how visitors use our website. Data collected is aggregated and anonymized where possible. For more information, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We carefully select the third-party services we use and limit them to those that help us operate and improve our website.

6. How to Control Cookies

You have several options for controlling or disabling cookies:

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings menu. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block all cookies from specific websites.
  • Block all cookies entirely.

Instructions for managing cookies in the most common browsers can be found in each browser's help section:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Effect of disabling cookies

If you disable all cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly. Essential cookies, in particular, are needed for basic website features such as submitting forms or maintaining your session.

7. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating that you do not wish to be tracked. Because there is currently no industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, our website does not currently respond to DNT signals.

We do, however, minimize tracking on our website to what is strictly necessary for functionality and analytics.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use, our practices, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
