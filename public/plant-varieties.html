Plant Varieties Protection

Secure exclusive rights for your new plant varieties

What is Plant Variety Protection?

Plant Variety Protection (PVP), also known as Plant Breeders' Rights, grants breeders exclusive rights over new, distinct, uniform, and stable plant varieties.

It encourages agricultural innovation by ensuring breeders can recoup their investment in research and development. In Egypt, plant varieties are protected under Law No. 82 of 2002, which aligns with UPOV standards.

Protect Your Variety How It Works

Eligibility Criteria (DUS)

Distinct

Clearly distinguishable from any other known variety.

Uniform

Sufficiently uniform in its relevant characteristics.

Stable

Remains true to its description after repeated propagation.

Novel

New – not sold or marketed prior to application.

Plant Variety Protection Process

1
Eligibility Check

Assess DUS criteria and novelty.

2
Application Filing

Submit technical description to the relevant authority.

3
Examination

Official DUS testing and examination.

4
Grant & Maintenance

Certificate issuance and annual renewals.

Protection period: 20 years for most species, 25 years for trees and vines.
