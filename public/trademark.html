Trademark Registration

Protect your brand identity nationally and internationally.

Why Register Your Trademark?

Your trademark is your brand's identity. It distinguishes your products or services from competitors and builds customer trust. Registering your trademark gives you exclusive rights and legal protection against infringement.

Our Trademark Services Include:

  • Trademark search and clearance
  • Filing applications locally (Egyptian Trademark Office)
  • International registration via Madrid Protocol
  • Responding to office actions
  • Trademark renewal and maintenance
  • Opposition and cancellation proceedings
  • Trademark licensing and assignment

The Registration Process

Step 1
Search

Comprehensive search to ensure your mark is available.

Step 2
Application

Prepare and file the application with the relevant office.

Step 3
Examination

Official examination and response to objections.

Step 4
Registration

Certificate issuance and ongoing protection.

Why Work With Us?

Our team has extensive experience in trademark prosecution before the Egyptian Trademark Office and internationally. We provide strategic advice to maximize protection for your brand.
