Contracts & Agreements

IP Licensing & Technology Transfer

Commercializing your intellectual property through well-structured agreements

Licensing is how intellectual property becomes revenue. A well-drafted license agreement allows you to monetize your patents, trademarks, and copyrights while keeping ownership — but a poorly drafted one can cost you control of your most valuable assets.

Our firm drafts, negotiates, and enforces IP licensing agreements and technology transfer contracts across industries. From patent and trademark licenses to software licensing, know-how transfer, and franchise agreements — we structure deals that protect your rights and maximize the value of your IP.
