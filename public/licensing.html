Licensing & Contracts

Drafting, negotiating, and managing IP and commercial agreements.

Maximize the Value of Your Intellectual Property

Licensing and contracts are essential tools for commercializing your intellectual property and managing business relationships. Our team helps you draft, negotiate, and enforce agreements that protect your interests and maximize the value of your assets.

Our Licensing & Contract Services Include:

  • IP Licensing Agreements: Drafting and negotiating licenses for patents, trademarks, copyrights, and know-how.
  • Technology Transfer Agreements: Facilitating the transfer of technology between research institutions and commercial entities.
  • Commercial Contracts: Drafting and reviewing distribution, supply, agency, and joint venture agreements.
  • Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs): Protecting your sensitive information in business discussions.
  • Franchise Agreements: Structuring franchise relationships and drafting related documents.
  • Contract Review & Negotiation: Assisting with the negotiation of favorable terms and conditions.

Why Work With Us?

Our attorneys combine deep legal knowledge with a practical understanding of business needs. We help you structure agreements that achieve your commercial objectives while minimizing risks. Whether you are licensing your IP to a third party or entering into a complex commercial transaction, we provide clear, strategic advice.
