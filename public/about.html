Our Story

A Decade of Legal Practice

Walid Mohamed Abu Al-Ela Law Firm has been serving clients since 2016, when its founder — Walid Mohamed Abu Al-Ela — was admitted to the Egyptian Bar Association and began independent legal practice.

Over the past decade, the firm has grown into a trusted name in intellectual property, arbitration, and corporate law — serving clients ranging from innovative startups to established corporations across Egypt and beyond.

What sets the firm apart is a commitment to treating every case as if it were the first — combining deep legal expertise with a personal, direct approach. The founder is also a registered Master's researcher in Intellectual Property at the National Institute of Intellectual Property.
