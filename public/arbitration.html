International Arbitration

Expert resolution of cross-border commercial disputes.

Why Choose Arbitration?

Arbitration offers a confidential, efficient, and neutral forum for resolving international commercial disputes. Our team has extensive experience in arbitrations under all major rules and institutions.

Our Arbitration Services Include:

  • Arbitration agreement drafting and review
  • Representation in ad-hoc and institutional arbitrations (ICC, LCIA, UNCITRAL, CRCICA)
  • Emergency arbitrator proceedings
  • Interim measures and injunctions
  • Enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards
  • Mediation and other ADR methods

Why Work With Us?

With years of experience and a deep understanding of both civil and common law systems, we provide strategic advice and effective representation at every stage of the arbitration process.
