Common Questions About Arbitration

Arbitration offers confidentiality, party-appointed decision-makers with specialist expertise, procedural flexibility, and — through the New York Convention — cross-border enforcement of awards in over 170 countries. It is often faster and more predictable than multi-jurisdictional litigation.
